🚂 Welcome to the Heritage Collection

The Norfolk Southern Heritage Collection preserves each of Norfolk Southern's heritage locomotives as permanent museum exhibits.

Every exhibit contains documented catches, archived videos, photographs, railroad history, museum statistics, and curator's notes from the TrainsForever Museum Archive.

Select a heritage unit below to begin exploring the collection.
