📖 History

Norfolk Southern 1066 honors the New York Central Railroad as part of Norfolk Southern's Heritage Unit program.

Built as a GE ET44AC, the locomotive remains active in freight service while preserving the legacy of the New York Central Railroad.

The New York Central Railroad was one of America's most influential railroads, operating an extensive network throughout the Northeast and Midwest. Famous for its "Water Level Route" between New York City and Chicago, the railroad became known for high-speed passenger service, efficient freight operations, and iconic locomotives such as the 20th Century Limited.

In 1968, the New York Central merged with the Pennsylvania Railroad to form Penn Central. Although the railroad itself no longer exists, its legacy continues through Norfolk Southern Heritage Unit 1066, which proudly carries the classic New York Central-inspired black and gray paint scheme.
