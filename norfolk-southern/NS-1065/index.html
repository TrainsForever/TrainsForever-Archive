📖 Railroad Story

Norfolk Southern unveiled NS 1065 in 2012 as part of its Heritage Unit Program to honor the historic Savannah & Atlanta Railway. The locomotive carries the railroad's classic black, white, and gold paint scheme on a modern EMD SD70ACe, preserving its legacy while remaining an active part of today's freight network.

Rather than placing history inside a museum building, Norfolk Southern returned it to the main line. Every trip made by NS 1065 continues the story of the railroad it represents.

📝 Curator's Notes

I've documented NS 1065 several times over the years, and every encounter has been memorable. While photographs and videos preserve many of those moments, one memory stands above the rest.

While spending time around Norfolk Southern's Elkhart Yard with friends, I watched 1065 race through the yard leading an intermodal train. I never managed to take a photograph, but it's still one of my favorite memories connected to this locomotive.

Some railroad memories become photographs. Some become videos. Others simply become stories you'll never forget.
