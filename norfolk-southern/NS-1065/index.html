📖 The Story

NS 1065 was painted in 2012 as part of Norfolk Southern's Heritage Unit Program. It honors the historic Savannah & Atlanta Railway by bringing its classic black, white, and gold paint scheme back to life on a modern locomotive.

Unlike a museum display that stays in one place, NS 1065 is still out there doing the job it was built for. It leads freight trains across the Norfolk Southern system while carrying the history of the Savannah & Atlanta Railway everywhere it goes.

💡 Museum Note

A Heritage Unit is a modern locomotive painted to honor a historic railroad. Instead of preserving history inside a building, Norfolk Southern put it back on the main line.

📝 Curator's Notes

I've caught NS 1065 several times over the years, and it's always one I'm happy to see. It isn't the rarest heritage unit, but every time it shows up, it's worth chasing.

One memory stands out more than any photo I've taken. While spending time inside Norfolk Southern's Elkhart Yard with friends, I watched 1065 fly past on an intermodal train. I never got a picture of it, but it's still one of my favorite memories connected to this locomotive.

That's really what this museum is about. Some memories become photos. Some become videos. Others are just moments you never forget.
