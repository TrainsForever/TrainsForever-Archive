📖 Railroad History

Norfolk Southern 1065 honors the historic Savannah & Atlanta Railway, one of the many railroads represented through Norfolk Southern's Heritage Unit Program. Introduced in 2012, the locomotive carries the railroad's elegant black paint, bold white stripe, and gold lettering inspired by the original Savannah & Atlanta Railway.

Rather than preserving these railroads inside museum buildings, Norfolk Southern returned their identities to the main line. Every trip made by NS 1065 continues the legacy of a railroad whose history helped shape today's Norfolk Southern.

Today, NS 1065 serves as both a working freight locomotive and a moving tribute to one of America's historic railroads.

📝 Curator's Notes

Savannah & Atlanta has always been one of those heritage units I'm happy to see. I've documented it several times over the years, and every catch still feels just as exciting as the last.

One memory that has always stayed with me actually wasn't a documented catch. While spending time inside Norfolk Southern's Elkhart Yard with friends, I watched NS 1065 race past on an intermodal train. I never had the chance to photograph or record it, but it's one of those moments I still remember because not every great railroad memory ends with a camera.

That's one of the reasons I built the TrainsForever Museum. Some memories become photographs. Some become videos. Others simply become stories worth preserving.

👥 Railfan Companions

One of the biggest reasons this museum exists is because railfanning has never been something I've done alone. Some of my favorite memories weren't just about catching a rare locomotive—they were about who I was with when it happened.

Alex has been one of those people. We've been best friends for more than eleven years, and many of the memories preserved throughout this museum were experienced together.
