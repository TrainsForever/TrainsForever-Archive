📝 Curator's Notes

Norfolk Southern 1065 has always been one of the highlights of the Heritage Fleet. Every documented catch has added another chapter to this exhibit, but one memory stands above the rest.

Special Memory

While inside Norfolk Southern's Elkhart Yard with friends, NS 1065 unexpectedly passed by leading an intermodal train. Although there wasn't enough time to photograph or record the locomotive, the moment remains one of the most memorable experiences connected to this exhibit and deserves to be preserved alongside the documented catches.

📚 Railroad History

The Savannah & Atlanta Railway was a regional railroad that served Georgia for many decades, connecting communities and industries across the southeastern United States. Although it eventually became part of larger railroad systems, its legacy continues through Norfolk Southern Heritage Unit 1065.

Painted in the railroad's historic colors, NS 1065 serves as a rolling tribute to the Savannah & Atlanta Railway while introducing its history to a new generation of railroad enthusiasts.
