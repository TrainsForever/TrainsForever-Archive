Locomotive Information

Norfolk Southern 1067 commemorates the historic Reading Company, carrying the railroad's iconic green and yellow heritage paint scheme. Reading was one of the most recognizable railroads in the northeastern United States, known for both freight operations and famous passenger services. Today, NS 1067 keeps that legacy alive while serving as one of the most frequently documented heritage units within the TrainsForever Museum Archive.
