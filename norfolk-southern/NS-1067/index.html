📚 Railroad History

The Reading Railroad, officially the Philadelphia and Reading Railroad, became one of the most recognizable railroads in the eastern United States. Famous for its green and yellow paint scheme and its "Bee Line Service" branding, the railroad developed a strong reputation for efficient freight transportation.

Today, Norfolk Southern Heritage Unit 1067 carries the Reading colors across the modern railroad network, preserving the legacy of one of America's most iconic railroads for future generations.
