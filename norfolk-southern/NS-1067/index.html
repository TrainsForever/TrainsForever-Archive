Locomotive Information

Norfolk Southern 1067 commemorates the historic Reading Company, carrying the railroad's iconic heritage paint scheme. Reading was one of the most recognizable railroads in the northeastern United States and remains one of the most frequently documented heritage units within the TrainsForever Museum Archive.
