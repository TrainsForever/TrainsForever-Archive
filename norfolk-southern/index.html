🚂 Welcome to the Norfolk Southern Collection

Welcome to Gallery One of the TrainsForever Museum Archive. This collection is dedicated to preserving Norfolk Southern's heritage fleet, special interest locomotives, executive equipment, and rare railroad history through photographs, videos, documented catches, and museum exhibits.

Every locomotive featured in this gallery has its own permanent exhibit containing archive statistics, historical information, curator's notes, and featured media from the TrainsForever collection.

📝 Curator's Note

The Norfolk Southern Heritage Fleet is one of the most recognizable modern locomotive fleets in North America. Every exhibit in this gallery has been carefully created to preserve not only the history of each locomotive, but also the personal experiences, photographs, videos, and memories connected to every documented catch.
