📖 History

Norfolk Southern 1066 honors the New York Central Railroad as part of Norfolk Southern's Heritage Unit program. The locomotive wears the classic lightning-stripe-inspired black and gray paint scheme that pays tribute to one of America's most influential railroads.

Built as a GE ET44AC, NS 1066 remains in active freight service while honoring the legacy of the New York Central. The heritage locomotive commemorates the railroad's long history of passenger and freight service throughout the northeastern United States and has become a favorite among railfans.
